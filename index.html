Microscopy Reference Guide

Learn about the characteristics of different microorganisms.

1. Bacteria

Cocci: Spherical or round-shaped cells.

Bacilli: Rod-shaped cells.

Spirilla: Spiral-shaped cells.

Common arrangements: Single, pairs, chains, and clusters.

2. Protozoa

Single-celled eukaryotic organisms. Some move using cilia, flagella, or pseudopodia.

3. Algae

Photosynthetic organisms that may appear as single cells, colonies, or filaments.

4. Fungi

May appear as budding yeast cells or thread-like hyphae.

5. Microscopy Reminders

  • Record the magnification used.
  • Observe cell shape and arrangement.
  • Record the staining method.
  • Compare observations with reliable laboratory references.
  • Do not identify bacterial species based on shape alone.
